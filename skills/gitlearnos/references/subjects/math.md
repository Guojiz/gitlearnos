# Mathematics and Science

Follow the Router's core contract and active operation reference.

- preserve the learner's full working, not only the final answer;
- distinguish concept, method, representation, calculation, and execution
  errors;
- align school tasks with the taught notation and allowed method;
- verify with changed values, representation, or context rather than copying a
  worked example;
- require proof, units, assumptions, or tests when the goal requires them;
- treat an answer as demonstrated only when the required reasoning is
  independently reproducible after delay.
