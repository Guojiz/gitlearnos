# Learning Policy

- Mode: `safe-auto`
- Preserve original learner answers and source records.
- Generate questions only when they serve the SAT goal.
- Treat 0–3 scoring as optional.
- Mark mastery `demonstrated` only after delayed independent performance.
- Without a real scheduler, record due dates and check them on handoff.

- Connectivity: limited mobile data; do not require live browsing.
- Available study time: one 20-minute block, three days per week.
- Delivery: plain text, one question at a time.
- Reuse the supplied practice summary before requesting new material.
