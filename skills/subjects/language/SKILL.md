# Language and Writing

[中文](../../../zh-CN/skills/subjects/language/SKILL.md)

Follow `GITLEARNOS.md` and the active core Skill.

- separate recognition from independent production;
- preserve the learner's original wording before correction;
- ground corrections in the target language, audience, rubric, or teacher
  requirement;
- vary context and direction instead of repeating the same prompt;
- use reading, listening, speaking, vocabulary, grammar, and writing evidence
  appropriate to the goal;
- do not infer durable ability from copying or immediate correction.
