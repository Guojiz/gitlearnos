# Optional Spaced-Repetition Method

This is a subject method, not a universal GitLearnOS state machine. Use it only
for knowledge or skills that benefit from delayed retrieval or performance.
Open research, creative work, and projects may use rubrics, tests, artifacts,
or milestones instead.

## Optional 0–3 rubric

| Score | Observable result | Example next interval |
|---|---|---|
| 0 | no correct performance after substantial support | 1 day |
| 1 | correct only with major hints or imitation | 2 days |
| 2 | mostly correct with a minor hint or execution error | 4 days |
| 3 | independently correct; transfer succeeds when required | 7 days |

Intervals are policy defaults, not proof that a scheduler exists. Record an ISO
due date; create background delivery only through a verified scheduler.

## Mapping to core mastery

- `unknown`: no useful attempt;
- `learning`: supported, immediate, partial, or failed attempt;
- `demonstrated`: independent success after delay, with transfer when required.

A score may support a state but does not replace the evidence link. Repeated
success adds evidence to `demonstrated`; it does not create extra mandatory
mastery states.

## Review record

Keep the prompt, original learner answer, support used, feedback, optional
score, next check, and evidence links in one review record. Never overwrite the
answer or infer mastery from reading, completion, or immediate imitation.

## Selection

Prefer overdue goal-critical work, recent weak evidence, due open gaps, and
transfer checks. Keep a review small enough to complete. Generated but
unattempted questions remain `planned`.
